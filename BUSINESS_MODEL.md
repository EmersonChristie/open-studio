# Open Studio - Business Model & Pricing Strategy

## Subscription Plans

### 🆓 **Free Plan** - Testing & Evaluation
**Price:** $0/month
**Target:** Users wanting to test the platform

**Limits:**
- 5 artworks maximum
- 1 user only
- 1 gallery/organization
- 0.5GB image storage
- Basic inventory only
- Email support

**Purpose:** Allow potential customers to test core functionality before committing to a paid plan.

---

### 🎨 **Artist Plan** - Individual Artists
**Price:** $19/month or $190/year (17% discount)
**Target:** Individual artists managing their personal portfolio

**Limits:**
- 100 artworks maximum
- 2 users (artist + assistant/partner)
- 1 gallery/organization
- **Single artist constraint:** All artworks must be by the same artist
- 5GB image storage
- 500 monthly emails

**Features:**
- ✅ Basic inventory management
- ✅ Basic reporting
- ✅ Artist profile & portfolio view
- ✅ Client management
- ✅ Sales tracking
- ✅ Data export
- ✅ Bulk import
- ❌ Multiple artists
- ❌ Advanced reporting
- ❌ API access

**Purpose:** Perfect for individual artists who want to manage their portfolio, track sales, and manage client relationships.

---

### 🏛️ **Gallery Plan** - Art Galleries & Dealers
**Price:** $49/month for first gallery + $39/month for each additional gallery
**Target:** Art galleries, dealers, and institutions

**Limits:**
- 500 artworks per gallery
- Unlimited users per gallery
- Unlimited galleries (each adds $39/month)
- 50GB image storage per gallery
- Unlimited monthly emails

**Features:**
- ✅ Everything from Artist plan
- ✅ Multiple artists support
- ✅ Advanced reporting & analytics
- ✅ Staff management
- ✅ Exhibition management
- ✅ API access
- ✅ Priority support
- ✅ Custom branding
- ✅ Advanced integrations

**Pricing Examples:**
- 1 Gallery: $49/month
- 2 Galleries: $88/month ($49 + $39)
- 3 Galleries: $127/month ($49 + $39 + $39)

**Purpose:** Full-featured solution for galleries managing multiple artists, exhibitions, and complex operations.

---

## Key Business Model Features

### 🔄 **Upgrade Path**
```
Free (Test) → Artist (Portfolio) → Gallery (Business)
```

### 💰 **Revenue Scaling**
- **Artist Plan:** Fixed recurring revenue per artist
- **Gallery Plan:** Scales with business growth (more galleries = more revenue)

### 🎯 **Target Markets**
1. **Individual Artists:** Portfolio management, client tracking
2. **Small Galleries:** 1-2 location galleries with multiple artists
3. **Gallery Networks:** Multiple locations, complex operations
4. **Art Dealers:** Managing inventory across multiple venues

### 🚀 **Growth Strategy**
1. **Free tier** attracts users to test platform
2. **Artist plan** converts individual artists with portfolio needs
3. **Gallery plan** captures serious businesses with scaling pricing
4. **Per-gallery pricing** grows revenue as customers expand

### 🔒 **Plan Enforcement**
- **Artwork limits:** Hard limits prevent exceeding plan capacity
- **User limits:** Control access based on plan
- **Feature gates:** Advanced features only available on higher plans
- **Artist constraint:** Artist plan enforces single-artist rule

This model provides clear value propositions for each user type while creating natural upgrade incentives as users' needs grow.
